Quick installation card

Confirmed baseline: v0.9.0 · CC candidate: v0.10.0-alpha.1, hardware testing pending

  1. Back up. Copy the entire SD card off the module. Keep the original stock firmware.
  2. Choose the release. Default v0.9.0 is the confirmed baseline. v0.10.0-alpha.1 is an experimental CC candidate.
  3. Verify and build. Choose official stock v3.26 HEX in the firmware tool, wait for verification, then build.
  4. Connect the card root. Hold PROJ about five seconds to enter USB storage. Use Chrome/Edge on HTTPS.
  5. Write or download. Write firmware to SD card, or download and copy it yourself. Keep only one firmware HEX at the card root.
  6. Finish on the module. Wait for read-back verification, safely disconnect USB storage, then follow the module’s firmware-update procedure. Keep power stable.

Rollback

Put only your original official HEX, or retained confirmed baseline HEX, at the card root and use the normal module update procedure. Preserve your pattern backup: older firmware can discard embedded chord/CC data on save.
