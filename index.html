Copy with your file manager, then update

  1. Back up. Keep a full card backup and the original stock firmware.
  2. Open storage. Hold PROJ about five seconds, then open the card in Finder or File Explorer.
  3. Copy the HEX. Use the card root, outside project folders. Keep only one firmware HEX; remove any “(1)” suffix.
  4. Eject & update. Wait for copying, safely eject in your OS, then follow the module’s update procedure with stable power.

Release features & limitations

In this release

    Before installing

      Public versions remain below 1.0 until the complete experience passes hardware acceptance. CC automation belongs to the experimental candidate and remains untested on hardware.

      The editor includes DAW-style selection and drawing, undo/redo, Velocity and CC lanes, native single-file patterns, backups, recovery and SD-ready project ZIPs.

      Full features and release notes

      Compatibility & local processing

      Only the verified stock v3.26 image is supported. Already-patched and unknown firmware files are rejected. Building needs SHA-256 support on HTTPS or localhost. You supply the vendor firmware; this tool contains patch definitions only. Firmware and patterns stay on your computer. No files are uploaded.

      Build diagnostics & checksums

      Choose a stock firmware file to begin.

      Return to stock

      1. Keep an off-card backup and your original official v3.26 HEX.
      2. Put only that stock HEX at the card root and follow the module’s normal update procedure.
      3. Stock firmware plays native mono notes. Saving patterns from stock firmware removes embedded chord and CC data, so retain your backup.
